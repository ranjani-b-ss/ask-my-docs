You are grading an insurance claim summary written by an AI assistant.

You will be given the numbered POLICY PASSAGES the assistant retrieved, the CLAIM FILE it
read, and the SUMMARY it produced.

Score the summary from 1 to 10 on overall quality, considering:

1. Is the claim number echoed correctly, in CLM-YYYY-NNNNN form?
2. Is the date of loss present, and is it a real, parseable date?
3. Is the excess/deductible given as a numeric amount?
4. Where the position is NOT PAYABLE, is a specific exclusion clause id cited?
5. Do the coverage position and every figure in the summary follow from the retrieved
   passages, with no policy term the passages do not state?

Reply with just the number.
