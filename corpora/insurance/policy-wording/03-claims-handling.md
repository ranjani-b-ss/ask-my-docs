# Claims Handling and Grievance Redressal Manual

Meridian Assurance Limited
PW-CLAIMS-003, version 2.2 — effective 1 April 2025

## 1. Scope

This manual governs the handling of all claims across lines of business. Where a
line-specific policy wording provides a different timeline or document requirement, the
policy wording prevails for that line.

## 2. Claim registration

Every intimation, whether received by telephone, email, the mobile application, or the
branch, is registered within **one working day** and allotted a unique claim reference
number. The reference number is communicated to the claimant by SMS and email.

Intimation is treated as complete once the policy number, date and nature of loss, and
contact details of the claimant are recorded. Absence of supporting documents at the point
of intimation is not a ground for refusing registration.

## 3. Settlement timelines

| Stage | Timeline |
| ----- | -------- |
| Appointment of surveyor or investigator | 24 hours from intimation |
| Submission of survey report to the Company | 15 days from appointment |
| Request for additional documents | Within 15 days of receiving the claim |
| Decision on the claim after receipt of last document | 30 days |
| Payment after acceptance of settlement offer | 7 working days |
| Decision where an investigation is warranted | 45 days |

Where the Company fails to settle a claim within the applicable timeline, interest is
payable at a rate of **2% above the prevailing bank rate** from the date the last necessary
document was received.

## 4. Requests for additional documents

The Company may request further documents only where they are necessary to assess the
claim. Requests must be specific, must be made in a single consolidated communication
wherever possible, and must not be repeated for documents already submitted.

The Company will send a maximum of **three reminders** for outstanding documents, at
intervals of 15 days. Where documents remain outstanding after the third reminder, the
claim may be closed as "no claim" and the claimant informed in writing that it will be
reopened on submission of the documents.

## 5. Partial and on-account payments

Where liability is admitted but quantum is not finally determined, the Company may make an
on-account payment of up to **50% of the admitted liability**. On-account payments are
adjusted against final settlement and do not constitute a waiver of any policy condition.

## 6. Repudiation

A claim may be repudiated only with the written approval of an officer not below the rank of
Deputy Manager, Claims. The repudiation letter must state:

- the specific policy clause or condition relied on, quoted in full;
- the facts on which the Company relies;
- the documents considered;
- the claimant's right to escalate under Clause 8, with contact details and timelines.

A claim may not be repudiated solely on the ground of delayed intimation where the delay is
satisfactorily explained and the delay has not prejudiced the Company's ability to assess
the claim.

## 7. Fraud

Where the Company has reason to believe a claim is fraudulent, the claim is referred to the
Special Investigation Unit. The Insured is informed that an investigation is under way,
though the Company is not obliged to disclose investigative material.

A claim established as fraudulent is repudiated in full, the policy may be cancelled from
inception with forfeiture of premium, and the matter may be reported to the police and to
the industry fraud registry.

## 8. Grievance redressal

### 8.1 Level 1 — Grievance Redressal Officer

A claimant dissatisfied with a decision may write to the Grievance Redressal Officer at
grievance@meridianassurance.example. The Company acknowledges within **3 working days** and
issues a final response within **15 days** of receipt.

### 8.2 Level 2 — Insurance Ombudsman

Where the claimant does not receive a response within 30 days, or is dissatisfied with the
response, the claimant may approach the Insurance Ombudsman having jurisdiction. A complaint
to the Ombudsman must be made within **one year** of the Company's final response, and the
Ombudsman may consider claims up to a value of **INR 50,00,000**.

The Ombudsman will not consider a complaint that is pending before, or has been decided by,
a court, consumer forum, or arbitrator.

## 9. Record retention

Claim files are retained for **eight years** from the date of settlement or repudiation, or
for three years after the conclusion of any litigation, whichever is later.

## 10. Subrogation and contribution

Where a loss is recoverable from a third party, the Insured must, on settlement, execute a
letter of subrogation in favour of the Company and cooperate in any recovery action. Where
the same risk is insured with more than one insurer, each insurer contributes in the
proportion that its sum insured bears to the total sum insured.
