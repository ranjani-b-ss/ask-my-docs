# Private Car Package Policy — Section I: Own Damage

Meridian Assurance Limited
Policy wording PW-MOTOR-001, version 5.1 — effective 1 April 2025

## 1. What is covered

The Company will indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the insured vehicle
and its accessories caused by:

- accidental external means, including collision and overturning;
- fire, self-ignition, lightning, or explosion;
- burglary, housebreaking, or theft;
- riot, strike, or malicious act;
- terrorist activity;
- flood, inundation, storm, cyclone, hailstorm, earthquake, or landslide;
- damage in transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator, or air.

## 2. Basis of settlement

Claims are settled on the basis of **Insured Declared Value (IDV) less depreciation**,
subject to the sum insured stated in the schedule. The Company may, at its option, repair,
reinstate, or replace the vehicle or part, or pay the amount of the loss in cash.

### 2.1 Depreciation on parts

Unless a Nil Depreciation endorsement applies, the following rates of depreciation are
deducted from the cost of replaced parts:

| Part category | Depreciation |
| ------------- | ------------ |
| Rubber, nylon, plastic parts, tyres, tubes, batteries, airbags | 50% |
| Fibreglass components | 30% |
| Parts made of glass | Nil |
| All other parts — vehicle up to 6 months old | Nil |
| All other parts — vehicle 6 months to 1 year | 5% |
| All other parts — vehicle 1 to 2 years | 10% |
| All other parts — vehicle 2 to 3 years | 15% |
| All other parts — vehicle 3 to 4 years | 25% |
| All other parts — vehicle 4 to 5 years | 35% |
| All other parts — vehicle over 5 years | 50% |

### 2.2 Compulsory deductible

A compulsory deductible of **INR 1,000** applies to every own damage claim for vehicles
with a cubic capacity up to 1500cc, and **INR 2,000** for vehicles above 1500cc. The
deductible is in addition to any voluntary deductible chosen by the Insured and stated in
the schedule.

### 2.3 Total loss and constructive total loss

A claim is treated as a **total loss** where the vehicle is stolen and not recovered, or
where the aggregate cost of retrieval and repair exceeds **75% of the IDV**. In a total
loss settlement the IDV is paid in full, depreciation under Clause 2.1 does not apply, and
the salvage and registration certificate vest in the Company.

## 3. What is not covered

The Company is not liable for:

- normal wear and tear, ageing, and depreciation of the vehicle;
- mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure, or breakage;
- damage to tyres and tubes unless the vehicle is damaged at the same time, in which case
  liability is limited to **50% of the cost of replacement**;
- loss or damage caused while the vehicle is driven by a person without a valid and
  effective driving licence;
- loss or damage caused while the driver is under the influence of intoxicating liquor or
  drugs;
- loss or damage arising from the vehicle being used outside the limitations as to use
  stated in the schedule, such as a private car used for hire or reward;
- consequential loss, including loss of use, loss of earnings, and hire charges for a
  replacement vehicle;
- loss or damage occurring outside the geographical area stated in the schedule.

## 4. Claim intimation and procedure

The Insured must give **immediate written notice** to the Company of any loss, and in any
event within **48 hours** of the occurrence. Theft claims must additionally be reported to
the police without delay and the First Information Report number provided to the Company.

The vehicle must not be dismantled or repaired before the Company's surveyor has inspected
it, except to the extent necessary to move it to a place of safety or to comply with a
direction of the police.

### 4.1 Documents required

- completed and signed claim form;
- copy of the registration certificate and the driving licence of the person driving;
- original repair estimate and, after repair, the original invoice and payment receipt;
- police First Information Report for theft, third-party injury, or death;
- for total loss, the original registration certificate, both keys, and a letter of
  subrogation and indemnity on stamp paper.

### 4.2 Survey

The Company will appoint a surveyor within **24 hours** of intimation for vehicles within
municipal limits and within 48 hours elsewhere. The surveyor's report is advisory and does
not by itself constitute admission of liability.

## 5. Cashless and reimbursement

Where repairs are carried out at a garage in the Company's network, the Company settles
the admissible amount directly with the garage, and the Insured pays only the deductible,
depreciation, and non-admissible items. Where repairs are carried out at a garage outside
the network, the Insured pays the garage and claims reimbursement.

Reimbursement claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the date of the final repair
invoice.

## 6. No Claim Bonus

Where no claim is made or pending during the preceding policy year, a No Claim Bonus is
allowed on renewal on the own damage premium:

| Consecutive claim-free years | No Claim Bonus |
| ---------------------------- | -------------- |
| 1 | 20% |
| 2 | 25% |
| 3 | 35% |
| 4 | 45% |
| 5 or more | 50% |

No Claim Bonus is lost if a claim is made during the policy year, and is not transferable
to another person, though it is transferable by the Insured to a replacement vehicle.
