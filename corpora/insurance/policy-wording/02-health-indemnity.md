# Group Health Indemnity Policy

Meridian Assurance Limited
Policy wording PW-HEALTH-002, version 3.4 — effective 1 April 2025

## 1. Hospitalisation benefit

The Company will indemnify medical expenses reasonably and necessarily incurred for
in-patient hospitalisation of an Insured Person for a period exceeding **24 consecutive
hours**, up to the sum insured stated in the schedule.

Covered expenses include room rent, nursing charges, intensive care charges, fees of
surgeons, anaesthetists, and physicians, cost of anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation
theatre charges, surgical appliances, medicines and drugs, diagnostic materials, and the
cost of prosthetic devices implanted during surgery.

### 1.1 Room rent limits

Room rent is payable up to **1% of the sum insured per day** for a normal ward and **2% of
the sum insured per day** for intensive care, unless the schedule states otherwise.

Where the Insured Person occupies a room with a rent exceeding the eligible limit, all
associated medical expenses in that admission are payable in the **same proportion** as
the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent. This is known as proportionate
deduction.

## 2. Pre and post hospitalisation

Medical expenses incurred in the **30 days immediately before** admission and the
**60 days immediately after** discharge are payable, provided they relate to the same
condition for which hospitalisation was necessary.

## 3. Day care procedures

Procedures listed in Annexure A that require less than 24 hours of hospitalisation because
of technological advancement are payable. Cataract surgery, dialysis, chemotherapy,
radiotherapy, lithotripsy, and tonsillectomy are among the listed procedures. Treatment
taken in an out-patient department is not a day care procedure and is not payable.

## 4. Waiting periods

| Waiting period type | Duration |
| ------------------- | -------- |
| Initial waiting period, all illnesses except accident | 30 days |
| Specified illnesses and procedures listed in Clause 4.2 | 24 months |
| Pre-existing diseases | 36 months |
| Maternity benefit | 9 months |

### 4.1 Accident exception

The initial 30-day waiting period does not apply to hospitalisation arising from an
accident.

### 4.2 Specified illnesses subject to the 24-month waiting period

Cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia of all types, hydrocele, fistula and
fissure in ano, haemorrhoids, sinusitis, gallstones and renal stones, benign lumps and
cysts, varicose veins, and joint replacement other than as a result of accident.

## 5. Maternity benefit

Where the schedule states that maternity benefit applies, expenses for delivery are
payable up to **INR 50,000 for a normal delivery** and **INR 75,000 for a caesarean
section**, limited to the first two living children. A waiting period of 9 months from the
commencement of cover applies. Expenses for termination of pregnancy within 12 weeks are
not payable except where medically necessary to save the life of the mother.

A newborn is covered from **day 91** after birth on payment of additional premium and
intimation to the Company within 30 days of birth.

## 6. What is not covered

- treatment taken outside India, unless the schedule provides worldwide cover;
- cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless required to treat an injury, burn, or cancer;
- dental treatment, unless requiring hospitalisation following an accident;
- spectacles, contact lenses, and hearing aids;
- treatment of obesity and weight control, other than bariatric surgery meeting the
  criteria in Annexure B;
- infertility and assisted reproduction;
- self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, and injury arising from participation in a
  criminal act;
- treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse;
- expenses arising from war, invasion, or nuclear contamination;
- non-medical items listed in Annexure C, including gloves, administrative charges,
  telephone charges, and attendant comfort items.

## 7. Claims — cashless

For planned admissions at a network hospital, the Insured Person or the hospital must
request pre-authorisation from the third-party administrator at least **48 hours before
admission**. For emergency admission, the third-party administrator must be intimated
within **24 hours of admission**.

The third-party administrator will respond to a complete pre-authorisation request within
**60 minutes** for emergencies and within 6 hours for planned admissions. Pre-authorisation
is an estimate of admissible liability and is not a final settlement.

## 8. Claims — reimbursement

Where treatment is taken at a non-network hospital, or where cashless facility was not
availed, the Insured Person pays the hospital and claims reimbursement.

Documents must be submitted to the third-party administrator within **15 days of
discharge**:

- completed claim form signed by the Insured Person and the treating doctor;
- original hospital discharge summary;
- original itemised hospital bill and payment receipt;
- original investigation reports and prescriptions;
- original pharmacy bills supported by prescriptions;
- copy of the Insured Person's identity proof and the policy or health card;
- for accident claims, the police report or medico-legal certificate where applicable.

Claims received after **90 days from discharge** are not admissible, except where the
Insured Person establishes to the satisfaction of the Company that the delay was for
reasons beyond their control.

## 9. Co-payment

Where the schedule states that a co-payment applies, the Insured Person bears that
percentage of each admissible claim. A co-payment of **20%** applies to all Insured Persons
aged 61 years and above at the commencement of the policy year, unless waived by
endorsement.
