# Endorsement 1 to the Private Car Package Policy

Meridian Assurance Limited
Endorsement END-2026-01 — effective 1 April 2026

This endorsement amends the Own Damage Section of the Private Car Package Policy
(PW-MOTOR-001, version 5.1). Where this endorsement conflicts with PW-MOTOR-001,
**this endorsement prevails**. All other terms, conditions, and exclusions of
PW-MOTOR-001 remain in force.

## 1. Nil Depreciation cover — amendment to Clause 2.1

Where the schedule records that Nil Depreciation cover is in force, the depreciation table
in Clause 2.1 of PW-MOTOR-001 **does not apply**, and the full cost of replaced parts is
payable, subject to the following:

- Nil Depreciation applies only to vehicles up to **5 years** old from the date of first
  registration;
- a maximum of **two Nil Depreciation claims** are permitted per policy year; from the
  third claim onward the depreciation table in Clause 2.1 applies in full;
- Nil Depreciation does not extend to tyres, tubes, and batteries, on which depreciation of
  **50%** continues to apply;
- glass, plastic, and fibreglass parts are payable in full.

## 2. Amendment to Clause 2.2 — Compulsory deductible

The compulsory deductible in Clause 2.2 is revised as follows:

> A compulsory deductible of **INR 1,500** applies to every own damage claim for vehicles
> with a cubic capacity up to 1500cc, and **INR 3,000** for vehicles above 1500cc.

The previous amounts of INR 1,000 and INR 2,000 no longer apply. Any voluntary deductible
stated in the schedule continues to apply in addition.

## 3. Amendment to Clause 2.3 — Constructive total loss threshold

The constructive total loss threshold in Clause 2.3 is reduced from 75% to **70% of the
IDV**. A claim where the aggregate cost of retrieval and repair exceeds 70% of the IDV is
therefore treated as a total loss.

The remaining provisions of Clause 2.3 are unchanged: the IDV is paid in full, depreciation
does not apply, and salvage and the registration certificate vest in the Company.

## 4. Amendment to Clause 4 — Intimation window

The intimation requirement in Clause 4 is amended from 48 hours to **72 hours** of the
occurrence. The requirement to report theft to the police without delay is unchanged.

## 5. New Clause 4.3 — Digital survey

The following clause is inserted after Clause 4.2:

> **4.3 Digital survey.** For own damage claims where the estimated loss does not exceed
> **INR 50,000**, the Company may conduct a digital survey by requiring the Insured to
> upload photographs and a short video of the damage through the mobile application, in
> place of a physical survey. A digital survey decision is communicated within **4 working
> hours** of a complete upload. The Insured may decline a digital survey and require a
> physical survey.

## 6. Amendment to Clause 5 — Reimbursement window

The window for submitting reimbursement claims in Clause 5 is extended from 30 days to
**45 days** from the date of the final repair invoice.

## 7. New Clause 6A — No Claim Bonus protection

The following clause is inserted after Clause 6:

> **6A.** Where the schedule records that No Claim Bonus Protection is in force, the first
> own damage claim in a policy year does not affect the No Claim Bonus on renewal. A second
> or subsequent claim in the same policy year results in the No Claim Bonus reverting to
> nil. Windscreen-only claims are disregarded entirely for No Claim Bonus purposes.

## 8. Unchanged provisions

Clause 1 (What is covered), Clause 3 (What is not covered), Clause 4.1 (Documents
required), and the No Claim Bonus scale in Clause 6 are unchanged. In particular, the
exclusion of consequential loss and the 50% limit on tyre replacement where the vehicle is
damaged at the same time remain in force.
