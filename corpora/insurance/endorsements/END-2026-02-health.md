# Endorsement 2 to the Group Health Indemnity Policy

Meridian Assurance Limited
Endorsement END-2026-02 — effective 1 May 2026

This endorsement amends the Group Health Indemnity Policy (PW-HEALTH-002, version 3.4).
Where this endorsement conflicts with PW-HEALTH-002, **this endorsement prevails**.

## 1. Amendment to Clause 4 — Waiting periods

The waiting period table in Clause 4 of PW-HEALTH-002 is deleted and replaced with:

| Waiting period type | Duration |
| ------------------- | -------- |
| Initial waiting period, all illnesses except accident | 15 days |
| Specified illnesses and procedures listed in Clause 4.2 | 12 months |
| Pre-existing diseases | 24 months |
| Maternity benefit | 9 months |

The previous durations of 30 days, 24 months, and 36 months no longer apply. The maternity
waiting period of 9 months is unchanged. The accident exception in Clause 4.1 is unchanged.

## 2. Amendment to Clause 1.1 — Room rent and proportionate deduction

Room rent limits in Clause 1.1 are revised to **2% of the sum insured per day** for a normal
ward and **4% of the sum insured per day** for intensive care.

**Proportionate deduction is abolished.** The second paragraph of Clause 1.1 is deleted.
Where an Insured Person occupies a room exceeding the eligible rent, only the excess room
rent itself is deducted; all other medical expenses in that admission are payable in full.

## 3. Amendment to Clause 2 — Pre and post hospitalisation

The pre-hospitalisation period is extended from 30 days to **60 days** before admission. The
post-hospitalisation period is extended from 60 days to **90 days** after discharge.

## 4. Amendment to Clause 9 — Co-payment

The age at which the mandatory co-payment applies is raised from 61 to **66 years**, and the
co-payment percentage is reduced from 20% to **10%** of each admissible claim.

## 5. Amendment to Clause 8 — Reimbursement submission window

The window for submitting reimbursement documents in Clause 8 is extended from 15 days to
**30 days from discharge**. The outer limit for admissibility is extended from 90 days to
**120 days from discharge**, subject to the same discretion where delay is beyond the
Insured Person's control.

## 6. Amendment to Clause 7 — Pre-authorisation response time

The third-party administrator's response time for a complete pre-authorisation request is
reduced from 60 minutes to **30 minutes** for emergencies, and from 6 hours to **4 hours**
for planned admissions. The requirement to request pre-authorisation 48 hours before a
planned admission is unchanged.

## 7. New Clause 3A — Modern treatments

The following clause is inserted after Clause 3:

> **3A. Modern treatments.** The following are payable up to **50% of the sum insured** per
> policy year: robotic surgery, oral chemotherapy, deep brain stimulation, immunotherapy
> using monoclonal antibodies, intravitreal injections, bronchial thermoplasty, and stem
> cell therapy for haematological conditions. These are payable whether administered on an
> in-patient or day care basis.

## 8. New Clause 5A — Newborn cover from birth

The following clause is inserted after Clause 5:

> **5A.** A newborn child of an Insured Person is covered from **the date of birth**, in
> place of day 91 as stated in Clause 5, provided the birth is intimated to the Company
> within **30 days** and additional premium is paid. Cover for the newborn is limited to
> **INR 2,00,000** until the next renewal, after which the full sum insured applies.

## 9. Unchanged provisions

Clause 6 (What is not covered) is unchanged in its entirety. In particular, treatment
outside India without a worldwide cover endorsement, cosmetic surgery, infertility
treatment, and the non-medical items in Annexure C remain excluded. The list of specified
illnesses in Clause 4.2 is unchanged, only its waiting period duration is reduced.
