Endorsement 3 to the Claims Handling Manual

Meridian Assurance Limited
Endorsement END-2026-03 — effective 1 June 2026

This endorsement amends the Claims Handling and Grievance Redressal Manual (PW-CLAIMS-003, version 2.2). Where this endorsement conflicts with PW-CLAIMS-003, this endorsement prevails.

1. Amendment to Clause 3 — Settlement timelines

The timeline table in Clause 3 is deleted and replaced with the following:

StageTimeline
Appointment of surveyor or investigator12 hours from intimation
Submission of survey report to the Company10 days from appointment
Request for additional documentsWithin 7 days of receiving the claim
Decision on the claim after receipt of last document15 days
Payment after acceptance of settlement offer3 working days
Decision where an investigation is warranted30 days

The interest provision in Clause 3 is amended: interest for delayed settlement is payable at 3% above the prevailing bank rate, in place of 2%, from the date the last necessary document was received.

2. Amendment to Clause 4 — Reminders for outstanding documents

The number of reminders for outstanding documents is reduced from three to two, at intervals of 10 days in place of 15 days. The provision that a closed claim is reopened on submission of the documents is unchanged, and closure must still be communicated in writing.

3. Amendment to Clause 5 — On-account payments

The ceiling for on-account payments is increased from 50% to 75% of the admitted liability. On-account payments continue to be adjusted against final settlement and continue not to constitute waiver of any policy condition.

4. Amendment to Clause 6 — Repudiation authority

Repudiation now requires the written approval of an officer not below the rank of Manager, Claims, in place of Deputy Manager. The four mandatory contents of a repudiation letter listed in Clause 6 are unchanged, and the bar on repudiating solely for delayed intimation remains in force.

5. New Clause 3A — Digital claim submission

3A. Claimants may submit all claim documents through the mobile application or the claims portal. A document uploaded digitally and legible is treated as an original for the purposes of this manual, and the Company may not require a physical original except where a statute requires it, or for a letter of subrogation, a stamped indemnity bond, or an original registration certificate in a total loss claim.

Where documents are submitted digitally, the Company acknowledges receipt automatically and the 15-day decision clock in Clause 3 starts from the timestamp of the upload.

6. Amendment to Clause 8.1 — Grievance response time

The Grievance Redressal Officer's final response time is reduced from 15 days to 10 days from receipt. The acknowledgement time of 3 working days is unchanged. The escalation route to the Insurance Ombudsman in Clause 8.2, the one-year limitation, and the INR 50,00,000 ceiling are unchanged.

7. Unchanged provisions

Clause 2 (Claim registration, one working day), Clause 7 (Fraud), Clause 9 (Record retention, eight years), and Clause 10 (Subrogation and contribution) are unchanged.
